Privacy Policy

Tink & the Chime Garden and Chime Garden Uploader · Last updated October 30, 2026

This policy covers the Tink & the Chime Garden website and Chime Garden Uploader, a private tool the show's creator uses to publish episodes to the show's own YouTube channel.

Use of YouTube API Services

Chime Garden Uploader uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the tool

Only the show's creator signs in to Chime Garden Uploader, with the Google account that owns the Tink & the Chime Garden YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What information it accesses and why

  • Permission to manage the creator's own YouTube channel (granted through Google sign-in), used only to upload the show's episodes, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, "made for kids" and AI-content settings, and schedule when they go public.
  • The channel's own list of uploaded videos, read only to find the episode being scheduled.

It does not access, collect or store information about viewers or any other YouTube users, and it does not use data for advertising.

Storage and sharing

The Google sign-in token and a list of the episodes it has scheduled (video IDs and publish times) are stored only on the creator's own computer. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and no data is sent anywhere except to YouTube to publish the episodes.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Locally stored data is deleted by removing the tool's files from the computer.

This website and children's privacy

This website does not use cookies, analytics or forms and collects no personal information. Our episodes are made for children and are designated "made for kids" on YouTube; we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Watching on YouTube is subject to YouTube's own policies.
